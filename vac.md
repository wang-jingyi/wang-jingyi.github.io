## Prospective PhD students

I am always looking for self-motivated students from a range of backgrounds to
push the boundary of formal methods towards more trustworthy AI and more secure
systems. The group encourages research with either theoretical depth or
practical impact, across formal methods, software engineering, AI/ML and
security.

**What helps.** A strong background and interest in mathematics and logic —
and, more importantly, a genuine appetite for doing research.
